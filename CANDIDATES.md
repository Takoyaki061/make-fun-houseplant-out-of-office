## Critique

- **A1** — "we're even" is a nice reciprocity twist (neglect answered with
  neglect), but "Back when someone remembers I exist" reads awkwardly and
  the ending fizzles instead of landing.
- **A2** — Clean structure, good punch ("He doesn't care either"), cactus
  voice. But the list ("water, sunlight, and patience") is a generic
  build-up and "out of patience" is a mild office phrase, which criterion
  2 penalises.
- **A3** — Good voice ("stop pretending this is fine"), but it opens with
  "Thanks for your email", a stock auto-reply phrase, and "I'm a fern" is
  a weaker plant claim than A6's flat "I am a plant".
- **A4** — "Do not move me" is a nice monstera-with-opinions beat, but the
  desk/windowsill setup is wordy and the joke is more cute than funny.
- **A5** — "Photosynthesizing out of spite" is the single funniest phrase
  in the set, but the setup ("took the watering can's memory with them")
  is convoluted — the logic muddles before the punch lands.
- **A6** — The joke is structural: an auto-reply from something that
  cannot read email. "Dying quietly by the printer since February" is the
  most vivid, specific image in the set, and the neglect (criterion 3)
  drives the whole line. Every clause earns its place; nothing is a
  cliche. Strongest on criteria 1 and 2, the two that matter most.
- **A7** — "My roots have started a union" is a great punch, but the
  middle ("My last drink was in March") is flat, and the line leans on
  the punch alone. Runner-up.
- **A8** — "Water me first, then we'll talk" is a decent punch, but
  "holding this same leaf" is a weak image next to A6's printer.

## Pick

**A6** — "This is an automatic reply. I am a plant. I cannot read email.
I have been dying quietly by the printer since February." (119 chars)

It wins on the judge's top two criteria: it is unmistakably a plant's
out-of-office reply, and the joke surprises twice (a plant that cannot
read email sending an auto-reply; a plant dying quietly by the printer).
The plant's neglected life is the joke itself, not decoration, and it is
tight at 119 characters with no wasted words.
